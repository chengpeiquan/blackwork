# Content And Product Surfaces

Use this reference for blogs, documentation, article/list pages, portfolio content, and product explanation pages.

## Page Shell

- Reuse the shared Blackwork header, main gutter, footer, theme control, language control, and social-link patterns where they fit.
- Keep the header quiet: brand and primary navigation first, search and utilities second. Collapse navigation deliberately on narrow screens instead of squeezing every desktop action into one row.
- Use the standard content gutter for articles and lists. Use a fullscreen main only when the page intentionally contains full-width bands or an immersive primary surface.
- Keep long-form text at a readable line length. Side navigation and table of contents must yield to the article on small screens.

## Hierarchy

- Give each page one primary heading. Use section headings to organize the reading path, not to restate the page title.
- Inside compact cards or sidebars, use small, tight headings. Do not reuse article-scale or hero-scale typography inside panels.
- Keep supporting metadata visually quieter than titles and body copy.
- Use separators, whitespace, or full-width background bands to divide major sections. Avoid placing every section in a floating card.

## Repeated Content

- Cards work for independently actionable repeated items such as projects. Keep their internal slots consistent so titles, descriptions, tags, and actions align across the grid.
- Stabilize repeated card height only where comparison benefits from it; do not hard-code text height without accounting for locale differences.
- Put item actions together and keep icon actions visually equal. Give every icon-only link an accessible name and tooltip/title.
- Use badges for compact categories or states, not as decoration around ordinary prose.

## Product Pages

- Lead with the literal product or offer and its primary action. Keep descriptive value claims in supporting copy.
- Use relevant product screenshots or real output where users need to understand the product. Do not replace inspectable product evidence with decorative graphics.
- Prefer full-width content bands with a constrained inner column. Avoid card grids as the default structure for every feature section.
- Apply a restrained accent to identity, status, or calls to action; keep the rest of the interface anchored by Blackwork semantic neutrals.

## Responsive Review

- Preserve the reading/task order when sidebars disappear or move.
- Wrap action and metadata rows intentionally; do not shrink labels until they collide.
- Make tables and code blocks scroll within their content region rather than widening the whole page.
- Check translated strings and long titles at mobile width before accepting fixed heights or single-line layouts.
